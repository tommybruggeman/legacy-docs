# Chapter 25 — AI Governance

## Purpose

Artificial Intelligence augments decision-making within Legacy.

AI assists users by interpreting league state, explaining rules, evaluating scenarios, and providing strategic recommendations.

AI does not define league truth.

The deterministic platform remains the canonical authority for all league state.

---

# System Ownership

This chapter governs:

- AI responsibilities
- AI limitations
- Evidence requirements
- Recommendation boundaries

---

# Business Rules

## Rule 25.1 — Deterministic Authority

AI shall never create or modify league state.

AI consumes canonical league state and produces recommendations.

---

## Rule 25.2 — Evidence

Every recommendation shall be supported by available league evidence.

When evidence is incomplete, AI shall communicate uncertainty rather than fabricate conclusions.

---

## Rule 25.3 — Rule Compliance

AI recommendations shall comply with every applicable Rulebook chapter.

AI shall not recommend illegal league actions.

---

## Rule 25.4 — Explainability

AI shall explain the reasoning supporting every recommendation.

Reasoning shall be traceable to league rules and league data.

---

# Invariants

- AI does not own league state.
- AI cannot override deterministic rules.
- AI recommendations remain explainable.

---

# Canonical Principles

The platform determines truth.

AI explains truth.

AI never invents truth.
